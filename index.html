Ashfen Hollow

Druid prototype v0.3: Gate Road and the edge of the Sunken Graveyard.
  • Move: tap the ground to walk there, or drag anywhere to steer.
  • Spell pad (bottom corner): tap the center for your main spell, or flick toward any spell on the ring (tapping it works too).
  • Tap an enemy to target it and cast your Tap spell.
  • Spells with a cast time need you to stand still. Instant spells work on the move.
  • Kite: snare it, rot it, walk away, nuke it.
  • Arrange lets you drag spells around the pad. Spells swaps in other spells. Settings has classic buttons and left-handed mode.

Spellbook

Drag a spell onto a slot, or tap a spell and then tap a slot. Slots swap if the spell is already loaded.
