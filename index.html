Ashfen Hollow

Druid prototype v0.6.1: Gate Road and the edge of the Sunken Graveyard.
  • Move: tap the ground to walk there, or drag anywhere to steer. Settings has a hold-to-move option and a visible stick.
  • Spell pad (bottom corner): tap the center for your main spell, or flick toward any spell on the ring (tapping it works too).
  • Tap an enemy to target it and cast your Tap spell.
  • Auto-cast (on by default) keeps firing your main spell at the enemy you're fighting, so your thumb is free to kite. Toggle it with the Auto pill above the pad.
  • Spells with a cast time need you to stand still. Instant spells work on the move.
  • Kite: snare it, rot it, walk away, nuke it. Higher-level enemies resist more and shrug off roots sooner.
  • Pulled too many? Run back to the gate guards. Tap people and signs to talk to them.
  • Tap your Druid frame (top left) for your Journal of kills and named mobs.
  • Arrange lets you drag spells around the pad. Spells swaps in other spells. Settings has classic buttons and left-handed mode.

Spellbook

Drag a spell onto a slot, or tap a spell and then tap a slot. Slots swap if the spell is already loaded.
