Loadout

  • Move: drag anywhere to steer. Settings → Movement also has Tap to walk and Hold to walk.
  • Fight: tap an enemy to target it. Your 8 spells sit in the grid at the bottom; the top-left one is your main attack. ◀ Back and Next ▶ above the grid switch targets.
  • 1st hit and Auto (under your frame): 1st hit casts your main attack when you tap an enemy; Auto keeps casting it so your thumb is free to move.
  • Spells with a cast time need you to stand still. A spell tapped while another is casting is queued (gold ring and a Next chip).
  • Rest: no potions. Health and mana come back fast out of combat, faster with Sit (left side). Pet classes also get Attack and Back off there.
  • Higher-level enemies resist more and shake off roots, snares and stuns sooner. Pulled too many? Run back to the guards.
  • Your frame (top left) opens gear, bags and the journal. Loot goes straight to your bags.
  • Log shows the full chat history. Menu has your spells, button layout and settings.
  • Where to go: the crossroads (levels 1–3), the rat tunnel under the sinkhole, then the Sunken Graveyard to the west and its crypt.
