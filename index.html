Ashfen Hollow

Druid prototype: Gate Road and the edge of the Sunken Graveyard.
  • Tap the ground to move. Tap an enemy to target it.
  • Cast with the ability buttons. Moving cancels a cast.
  • Kite: root it, rot it, walk away, nuke it.
  • Name colors show difficulty: green easy, blue a bit easier, white even, yellow and red dangerous.
  • Sit to recover faster. Getting hit stands you up.
