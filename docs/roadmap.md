# Phase 2 roadmap: the solo prototype

Goal: find out whether the Druid is fun to play through all of Zone 1. Four milestones, each ending with a check you can feel in your hands. "Sessions" are rough counts of one-feature work sessions.

| # | Milestone | What gets built | Done when | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Controls and core combat | Spell pad, loadouts, 6 Druid spells, Gate Road mobs | You can fight and kite comfortably on your phone | Done (v0.3) |
| 1 | Feel and polish | Number tuning from play-tests, sound effects, scenery depth fix, Old Gnawbone's summon, a simple minimap | A 15-minute session on Gate Road feels good, not just functional | 4–6 |
| 2 | Loot and character | Loot drops, inventory, 8 gear slots, 3 stat points per level, coin, a vendor | Kill, loot, equip, feel stronger: the loop works | 6–8 |
| 3 | All of Zone 1 | Wolfwood Edge, the Mire, Ruined Chapel; wolves, wisps, lurkers, cultists; Greyfang and Brother Malrec; day and night | Levels 1–10 play start to finish in about 3–5 hours | 8–12 |
| 4 | Make it a game | Hub city stub (trainer, vendor, bank), 3–5 quests, Thorn Aura at 10, full save of gear and bags, a start screen | A friend can pick it up from a link and play the whole zone without help | 5–8 |

## Gate review (after milestone 4)
Share the link with 5–10 friends, collect feedback with a short form, then decide:
1. Is combat fun?
2. Stay on web (Phaser + Capacitor) or move to Godot?
3. Start phase 3 (server and multiplayer)?

## Decisions needed along the way
- [ ] Before milestone 3: setting and working title, so zone names and quests are written once
- [ ] Before milestone 4: how much story and quest text versus pure camping and exploring
- [ ] At the gate review: web or Godot for the long run

## Not in phase 2
Multiplayer, accounts, crafting, auction house, other classes, monetization.
