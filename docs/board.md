# Board

One place for the work: what's being built now, what's next, every idea, and what shipped. The game design lives in `design.md`; this file is the flow.

## How we work (lean)
- **One thing at a time (WIP = 1).** Finish, test, ship, then pull the next card.
- **Big builds are fine (owner, Nov 19).** A card can be a whole system plus its content in one release. Each release still ships to the live link with test steps so it can be judged on a phone right away.
- **Pull, don't push.** Work comes from Now/Next. New ideas go to the Idea backlog first, never straight into the build.
- **Definition of done:** works in a headless test with no errors → docs updated (this board, `design.md` if rules changed) → committed and pushed → owner plays it on the phone.
- **Feedback loop:** play-test notes become cards. Defects get fixed before new features.
- **Prioritize by value ÷ effort.** Value = does it make the core loop more fun or answer an open question. Effort = S (one session), M (2–3), L (4+).

## Roadmap (phase 2: solo prototype)
| # | Milestone | Done when | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Controls and core combat | You can fight and kite comfortably on your phone | Done (v0.6) |
| 1 | Basic loot | Kills feel rewarding between dings | Done (v0.7) |
| 2 | First dungeon | A run is fun enough to repeat | Done (v0.9) |

| 3b | All 14 classes at baseline, in two batches of 5, on one default skin + testing grounds | Every class plays like itself and the sim can run them all | Done (v0.18), pending owner play-test |
| 3c | Balance (overnight sim runs) | All classes roughly even in the sim, anchored on Necro (easiest solo). Cleric may stay near-unkillable but slow; Warrior becomes the ultimate tank | Now (after the play-test) |
| 3d | Art redo: mock-ups, lock one character, write the style guide, roll it out to every class and the world | Everything follows one look | Later |
| 4 | Polish and gate review | A friend can play from a link without help | Later |

Gate review after milestone 4: 5–10 friends play, then decide whether the core is fun, web vs Godot, and whether to start phase 3 (multiplayer).

## Decisions
- **Two kinds of dungeon, two ways out (owner, Nov 27, map chat):** **open-world dungeons** (shared, part of the world: the crypt, Gnawbone's den under the rat tunnel) have **no teleport**: you walk out, the way you would anywhere in the world, free to leave at any point (the crypt's back tunnel to Wolfwood stays as an early peek at the next area). **Instanced dungeons** (your own copy: Dungeon 1 and every later instance) let you **teleport out once the boss is dead** (back to the dungeon's door), so a cleared run never makes you walk back. Druid and Wizard Gate keep working anywhere. Card "Ways out of dungeons".
- **Fear is afraid of everything (owner, Nov 27, v0.104):** a feared mob zig-zags away from you (new heading every 1–1.5 s) and never runs into another camp, so fear never pulls an extra pack. Unlike WoW, on purpose: kinder on a phone and for new players. Boxed in by camps, it cowers. Knobs in `js/path.js` (`FEAR_*`).
- **Only fear is afraid of everything (owner, Nov 27):** wolves running at low health and the Pale Rat calling for help still drag in idle mobs they pass. That risk stays on purpose.
- **Screen shake is for story (owner, Nov 27):** no shake on ordinary hits or boss attacks; save it for moments that tell a story (a boss entrance, the bell tolling by itself, a boss vanishing).
- **No count badges on mobs (owner, Nov 27):** a "x4" over a stack breaks immersion; show crowds with animation and placement instead.
- **Old Zone is frozen (owner, Nov 27):** the first build (Gate Road, guards, the first crypt) stays reachable only from DEV → Old Zone, to compare then and now. It is not part of the game: no chat works on it, checks it, or counts it in a pass.
- **The game never pauses (owner, Nov 20):** it's an online game, so the world keeps running. Anything that needs the player (a skill pick, a level-up choice) shows as a flash or marker on its button (e.g. Skills) for them to open when they want, never a window that stops play. Build new things this way; existing pausing menus get changed in the UI pass.
- **Portrait (phone held upright).** Fits short sessions, playing in bed, and one-thumb targeting. Seeing more of the world comes from zooming out, not turning the phone.
- **Zoom:** farthest (and default) view is about 215–235 game pixels wide on most phones, tuned by play-test (v0.11.5's ~290 was too far, v0.11.6's ~195 too close). Players pick Near / Far or pinch.
- **Screen layout (v0.12.1):** max 8 ability buttons on screen: 6 on the right (pad or classic) plus slots 7–8 on the left, in joystick and tap-to-move alike. Left column above the chat box: slot 7, slot 8, Sit (kept on screen: Necros sit while the pet and DoTs fight). Top left under the portrait: 1st hit / Auto. Top right: Menu (Spells, Arrange, Settings). No bottom bar. Character = tap the portrait. Target button dropped; Next ⟳ does that job.
- **Build wide (Nov 17):** all 14 classes reach baseline in batches of 5 on one default skin before balance, art or feel work. Supersedes "one class per release" for this phase.
- **Rest and respawn (Nov 18, owner):** no health or mana potions; instead fast regen out of combat (5%/s standing, 12%/s sitting). Respawns 2 min for mobs, 5 min for named and bosses while the game is solo; revisit in phase 3 when players share zones.
- **Spell grid (Nov 18, owner):** EverQuest-style 2 × 4 grid with all 8 spells in one place, same size buttons so every spell is equally easy to hit; players arrange their own order. Target buttons: Back and Next, which cycle the same nearest-first list in opposite directions (v0.29.1). First spells on top, main top left, grid size picked in Settings (v0.29.1). Left side kept free for pet controls later (Sit stays there for now). Supersedes the v0.12.1 left column for slots 7–8.
- **UI look for now (Nov 18, owner):** minimal, EverQuest-style (plain grey panels, thin borders, square buttons, plain text); the theme comes later. UI breakdowns are rough, one screen part at a time, list and notes in `docs/research/ui.md`.
- **Queued spell look (Nov 18, owner):** gold means "your move": a spinning gold ring on the button plus a "Next" chip under the cast bar. Movement is reserved for queued (no other button state moves). Tapping the queued spell again cancels it; a different spell swaps in.
- **One boss health bar:** bosses use the normal target frame (top right). The separate wide boss bar was removed in v0.14.2 as redundant.
- **Launch six (Nov 17):** Warrior, Cleric, Druid, Enchanter, Rogue, Necromancer. Enchanter replaces Wizard (more unique); Wizard moves to later.
- **Balancing waits** until most launch classes exist. Until then, only fix obvious outliers.
- **Rogue Evade is damage (Nov 17):** Evade, then Backstab right away. Most players would use it that way; the sim now runs only this version.
- **Pull singles at level 1 (Nov 17):** losing two skeletons at level 1 is fine (EverQuest-style); no fix needed there.
- **Boss 1 yardstick (Nov 17):** a class should beat the level-7 Boss 1 by level 8. Level 9 is borderline; level 10 (the cap) or never means the class gets a different look for soloing.
- **Soloable, at different difficulty by class:** everything can be soloed, but pet classes (Necro) solo comfortably while tanks (Warrior) find it harder and are rewarded for grouping. Tell players this when they pick a class.

- **Bard solos bosses by kiting (Nov 17, owner):** "slow but safe": Song of Travel + Dirge, Dirge doubled. Boss 1 takes about 4 minutes at level 8 with almost no risk.
- **Shaman DoTs get a small stacking bonus (Nov 17):** owner reversed "no bonus": each Shaman DoT ticks 10% harder for every other DoT on the same mob. Starting small; the balance pass can raise it.
- **Spirit of the Wolf is travel only (Nov 18, owner):** no speed in fights; out-running a boss with it gave Shamans too much. Slowing a boss's spells belongs to the Enchanter alone.
- **Warrior tools (Nov 17, owner):** Hold the Line + Last Stand (options d and b from the backlog card).
- **Cleric stays tough, Warrior gets tougher (Nov 17):** a Cleric that almost never dies is fine because its kills are slow. The Warrior should be the ultimate tank: the hardest class in the game to kill. Fixed in the balance pass, not now.
- **Art direction (Nov 17, owner):** characters go with **style B** from the mock-ups (top-left light, hue-shifted shading, black outline). **Low ground fog** is the signature effect, and it changes by area. Zone 1 for now: a town, critters in the forest, a crypt nearby. Monsters get the same template treatment **before** any art rollout.
- **Character look (Nov 17, owner, character chat):** **A+B mix, take 1 (normal heads)** from the character-looks canvas: A's approachable faces and colours with B's 12×18 height, gold and candlelight; robes with real detail (mantle, trim, belt, sash, hem, boots that walk). B's judgement ray is the spell template. **Faces** (Claude's call, owner OK): shown on Druid, Warrior, Cleric, Paladin, Ranger, Monk, Bard, Wizard, Magician, Shaman (war paint); hidden on Rogue (scarf), Necromancer (shadow, glowing eyes), Shadow Knight (closed helm, glowing eyes), Enchanter (porcelain illusion mask). **Everyone starts in rags and sticks**, and late-game players must look very different from new ones (silhouette, not just colour). How gear and cosmetics share that job: see the gear-look card (owner to pick).
- **Character size final (Nov 21, owner, character chat): 15 × 18.** Players stay the size they are in the game today (owner: "I like what we currently have"). History: 20 × 24 was picked on Nov 18 and shipped in v0.25, then v0.27.2 shrank it to 15 × 18 because 20 × 24 read too big next to doors. Trees, gravestones and tiles stay their current size. Humanoid monsters and NPCs get sized against 15 × 18 in the character design pass.
- **Rags in the game first (Nov 17, owner, character chat):** the Necromancer ladder looks good; put only **tier 0 (rags)** in the game now for every class, in the A+B take 1 look. - **v0.80** **Tap to walk finds its way** (optimization lane, item 2).
