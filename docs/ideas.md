# Ideas backlog

Ideas to revisit later. Not scheduled yet; most belong to phase 3 (multiplayer) or later.

## Loot
- **Personal loot:** every player gets their own loot from a kill, so there's no fighting or rolling over drops.

## Mob stealing and crowding (multiplayer)
- **Get players into dungeons quickly** so other people can't steal their mobs. Instanced group dungeons (1–5 players) are already in the design; the idea is to make them reachable early.
- **High-level players can't attack low-level mobs**, so they can't farm or grief newbie areas. Open question: how strict (block attacks entirely, or allow it with no XP or loot).
- Other options to consider later: first-hit tagging (the first player or group to hit a mob owns it), more spawns or extra zone copies when an area is crowded.
